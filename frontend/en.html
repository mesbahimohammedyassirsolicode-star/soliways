About SOLIWAYS

An event to explore the world of digital technology

SOLIWAYS is an initiative organized by SOLIWAYS, focused on IT, learning, and knowledge sharing. Over several days of activities, the event highlights different areas of the digital world and offers formats to learn, discover, and create.

Our Vision

Making SOLIWAYS a space for meeting and discovering digital technologies.

From workshops to expert talks, meetups and the web development hackathon, SOLIWAYS encourages discovering technologies and putting knowledge into practice.
